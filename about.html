About Grace Community Church

Building faith, hope, and community since 1985

Our Mission

To know Christ and make Him known through worship, biblical teaching, fellowship, and service, transforming lives and communities with the love of Jesus.

Our History

1985 - The Beginning

Grace Community Church was founded by a small group of families passionate about creating a welcoming, Bible-centered church in our community.

1990 - First Building

As our congregation grew, we moved into our first permanent building, allowing us to expand our ministries and serve more families.

2000 - Global Outreach

We launched our missions program, partnering with missionaries in 10 countries and making a global impact for the Gospel.

2010 - Community Center

We opened a community center to serve our neighborhood with food assistance, job training, and youth programs.

Today - Growing Strong

With over 500 members, we continue to grow in faith and service, always staying true to our mission of knowing Christ and making Him known.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God, without error in its original writings.

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, and glorious return.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works or human effort.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and mission.
