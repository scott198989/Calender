About Berean Baptist Church

A Bible-believing, King James Version church in White House, Tennessee

Our Pastor

Pastor Joseph Lang

Pastor and Mrs. Joseph Lang met in graduate school at Pensacola Christian College in the summer of 1999 and have enjoyed serving the Lord together at Berean Baptist Church of White House for over twelve years. They have nine children, including one in Heaven.

Pastor has his undergraduate in Pastoral Ministries, his Master of Divinity from Pensacola Theological Seminary, and two years' experience as an assistant pastor. For three years, he served under Dr. Shelton Smith at the Sword of the Lord Publishers. Pastor Lang came to White House in October 2008 to pastor Berean Baptist Church.

What We Believe

For a more detailed statement of faith, please contact Pastor Lang at (615) 681-0207.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God. We believe in the verbal and plenary inspiration of the Scriptures. It is inerrant, infallible, and preserved for every generation. We use the King James Version. The Bible is the full, final and sole authority for all faith and practice.

God

We believe in the one, true, Triune, living God who exists in three Persons—the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is eternal, omniscient, omnipotent, omnipresent, and self-subsistent. He created the world in six, twenty-four hour days.

Jesus Christ

We believe that the Lord Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, became man without ceasing to be God, having been conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary, in order that He might reveal God and redeem sinful men.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity. He convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment; bears witness to the truth; is the agent of the new birth; and seals, endues, guides, teaches, witnesses to, sanctifies, and helps the believer.

Salvation

We believe that salvation is the gift of God received by personal faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, Whose precious blood was shed for the forgiveness of our sins. We believe that all the redeemed, once saved, are kept by God's power and are thus secure in Christ forever.

Christ's Return

We believe in the personal, imminent, pre-tribulation, and pre-millennial coming of the Lord Jesus Christ for His redeemed ones, commonly known as the Rapture and in His subsequent return to earth, with His saints, to establish His millennial kingdom (after the tribulation).

The Church

We believe that the New Testament church is a local assembly of baptized believers who have voluntarily joined themselves together to carry out the Great Commission.

Our Ministries

Nursery Class

Ages: Newborn to Age 2

Our loving nursery workers provide a safe, clean environment where the youngest members of your family can learn about God and His great love for them.

Sunday School Classes

Ages: Age 3 to Grade 6

Children are taught Bible doctrine and Bible knowledge and are encouraged in Bible reading, prayer, and Scripture memorization.
