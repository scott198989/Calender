Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect on my first visit?

You can expect a warm welcome, contemporary worship music, and biblical teaching. Dress is casual - come as you are! Our services typically last about 90 minutes.

Is childcare available?

Yes! We offer nursery care for infants and children's programs for ages 2-12 during all services. All volunteers are background-checked and trained.

How can I get involved?

We have many opportunities to serve! Check out our Events page, attend a newcomer's class, or contact our office to learn about volunteer opportunities that match your interests and gifts.

Do you offer online services?

Yes, our Sunday services are livestreamed and archived for later viewing. Visit our website or contact us for links and details.
